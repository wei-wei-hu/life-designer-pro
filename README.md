# Life Designer Pro

Think through the life behind your next decision.

**[Open the Life Canvas](https://wei-wei-hu.github.io/life-designer-pro/)**: answer twelve plain questions, read or listen to a letter from your future self, see your life as a map, and save it all as a printable card. Free and private; nothing you type leaves your browser.

[![The Life Canvas](assets/canvas-preview.png)](https://wei-wei-hu.github.io/life-designer-pro/)

> If this future works, what would an ordinary day feel like for you?
