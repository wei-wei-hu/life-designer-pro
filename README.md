# Life Designer Pro

Think through the life behind your next decision.

**[Open the Life Canvas](https://wei-wei-hu.github.io/life-designer-pro/)**: answer twelve plain questions, talking or typing, and get back a letter from your future self, your life drawn as a map, and a printable card. Free and private; nothing you type or say leaves your browser.

[![The Life Canvas](assets/cover.png)](https://wei-wei-hu.github.io/life-designer-pro/)

## Why this exists

I built this for myself first, at my own crossroads. Most life-planning tools jump from a few answers to a confident recommendation. A promotion, a move, an exit: the decision in front of you is rarely the real question, and nobody can answer the real question but you. What helped me was slower: separate what you know from what you assume, sketch several futures instead of hunting for the one right answer, and test the smallest piece before committing to any of it.

Its central test is one question:

> If this future works, what would an ordinary day feel like for you?

A job tells you what you do. An ordinary day tells you what life you choose.

## How it thinks

- **Nothing is invented.** Every output is built only from your answers; "not sure yet" is a welcome answer, and blanks become open questions instead of guesses.
- **Several futures can be good.** You sketch three two-year roads: your current direction, an adjacent life, and the one you'd choose if money or image were no object. None is a backup plan.
- **Evidence beats deliberation.** One experiment small enough to start this week teaches you more than another month of thinking, and the letter points your next step at the evidence you still need.

## What you get

- **A letter from your future self**: key takeaways and recommendations in your own words, two years ahead. Read it, or listen to it.
- **Your life map**: your situation, question, roads, test, and protections drawn as one picture.
- **A printable card**: the letter inside, your map opposite, a cover that says who it's for. Print it for yourself, or fill it in for someone you love.
