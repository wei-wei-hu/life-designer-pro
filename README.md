# Life Designer Pro

Think through the life behind your next decision.

**[Open the Life Canvas](https://wei-wei-hu.github.io/life-designer-pro/)**: a free one-page tool that runs in your browser. Start with one question, fill twelve plain boxes (talk or type), then read or listen to a letter from your future self, see your life drawn as a map, and save both as a one-page PDF.

[![The Life Canvas](assets/canvas-preview.png)](https://wei-wei-hu.github.io/life-designer-pro/)

Its central test is simple:

> If this future works, what would an ordinary day feel like for you?

Everything you type or say stays in your browser. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.
