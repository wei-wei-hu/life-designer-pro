[ TODAY: the operating scope ]        [ TODAY: the craft ]

      what mix of operating, creating, teaching,

[ Enterprise builder ] [ Operator-educator ] [ Portfolio advisor ]

Reading the map: the two Today anchors hold the tension the session surfaced. The middle future carries the highlight only because it is being tested first, not because it won. The evidence loop is what re-scores all three futures over time.
