# Example Blueprint - Condensed and Fictional

> This shows tone and evidence discipline only. It deliberately omits most Core Blueprint sections; do not treat its structure as the deliverable spec: `references/DELIVERABLES.md` governs.

## Executive Summary

The user is a successful operations leader whose dissatisfaction comes primarily from spending too little time designing systems and developing people. Strong evidence supports a desire for practical creation, mentorship, and greater calendar control. Evidence about entrepreneurship and financial tolerance remains incomplete, so the recommended next step is a low-risk advisory-and-teaching prototype rather than resignation.

## The Real Question

**Original:** Should I leave my company?

**Deeper:** What mix of operating responsibility, creation, teaching, and autonomy produces a week I want to repeat?

## Top Weekly Conditions

1. Two protected creation blocks
2. Meaningful time with family
3. One opportunity to develop another person

## Three Paths

1. **Enterprise Builder:** Remain in a senior role with redesigned scope.
2. **Operator-Educator:** Combine leadership with executive teaching.
3. **Portfolio Advisor:** Advisory work, writing, and selective operating projects.

## Current Prototype

Teach one three-session internal workshop and advise one cross-functional team for 30 days. Measure energy, demand, family impact, and desire to repeat.

## Boundary

Do not accept a role whose normal schedule removes four evenings each week.

## Confidence

High confidence in the need for more creation and teaching. Moderate confidence in the portfolio path because income requirements and market demand still need testing.
